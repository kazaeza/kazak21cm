# Ear-training apps and tools for scale degrees, chord functions and keys (market survey, 2023-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-19. Method: WebSearch summaries plus WebFetch of primary pages (App Store listings, vendor pages). Version and date information is quoted as the page showed it; App Store pages often omit the year of the latest update, which is marked "year not shown". Reddit could not be fetched (WebFetch refuses reddit.com) and web search returned no r/eartraining threads, so no direct Reddit evidence is included. User-experience evidence is limited to App Store review excerpts and a few blogs. Several vendor blogs are promotional and are flagged as such.

## 1. What does each app/tool offer (drills, feedback, adaptivity, gamification, instrument link, pricing)?

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) tiny single-skill functional trainers (Functional Ear Trainer, Sonofield, StringKick) that put a single note against a cadence or drone, (b) broad curricula that include a functional/progression module (EarMaster, Perfect Ear, Harmonomics, Modalissimo, Toned Ear, OpenEar, ChordProg, Active Ear), and (c) a single product built around real-song chord progressions by degree (Hooktheory Chord Crush). Pricing clusters at free to about $20 one-time, or about $23-79 per year for subscriptions.

### Cited Findings

#### Pure functional / scale-degree trainers
- **Functional Ear Trainer** (iOS/Android; current mobile app by a different developer than the original Windows freeware by Alain Benbassat). Core idea: hear a cadence, then a note, name it in the key; letters, numbers or do-re-mi labels; difficulty grows from half scale to full scale to octave to multiple octaves and other scales; modes "Play!", "Melodic Dictations", "Listener Mode"; advanced mode plays two notes together; cadence and note volume set separately; custom levels (tonic, scale, cadence); stats can be cleared per level; daily reminders; intro game. App Store: version 3.32.15 (year not shown), 4.9/5 from 3.9K ratings, free with in-app purchase "Functional Ear Trainer Plus" $19.99. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926); original program description and per-note accuracy charts per [SourceForge/Softpedia-type listings](https://sourceforge.net/app/functional-ear-trainer/android/)
  - A vendor blog says it only plays a I-IV-V-I cadence then a single note and has "no chords, rhythm, dictation, or progress tracking"; this is contradicted by the App Store listing above (Melodic Dictations, Listener Mode, per-level stats). — [Fifths comparison, vendor-run, 2026-05/11](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/); contradicted by [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926)
  - Reviews: "doing 10-minutes a day has done wonders for my pitch" (2024); a bassist wants the app to detect notes played on the instrument instead of taps (2022); "limits its scope but delivers results" (2023). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926)
  - A Substack commenter describes a "random major" mode that moves to a new key with I-IV-V-I before each new note, which they found raised difficulty by forcing re-orientation to a new tonic (anecdotal). — [Substack comments](https://jerkmakesmusic.substack.com/p/how-to-develop-your-sense-of-pitch/comments)
- **Sonofield Ear Trainer** (iOS/iPad/Mac/visionOS, Sonofield Ear Trainer Education Ltd). Degrees Mode = single degree against a sustained drone (core); Melody Mode; Voice Mode (sing the requested degree into the mic); Pocket Mode (hands-free, random degrees and melodies, answer announced after each); Free Play instrument; progression path (Sun/Moon) with difficulty levels; custom presets for weak areas; colour-coded interface; game statistics (added 11/2025); cross-device Sync (05/2025); drone volume adjustable to zero; a melodic cadence on key changes that can be disabled. 4.9/5 from 397 ratings; free foundation, "Lifetime Pro" IAP at $14.99-$24.99 depending on listing; latest 1.10.1 (Jul 1, year not shown). Reviewers call the drone approach "musical and meditative" and say it fixes the problem of notes without tonic context. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sonofield-ear-trainer/id6740409139)
- **StringKick Scale Degree Ear Trainer** (web, free, no account). Backing drone sets key; identification mode and singing mode (pitch recognition checks the sung note); choose which degrees and major/minor/both; aimed at guitarists but the page lists no fretboard link; audio of singing stays on device. — [StringKick](https://www.stringkick.com/scale-degree-ear-trainer/)
- **Fifths** (web; vendor of the comparison article above, so self-described claims). Functional-first, scale degrees inside established keys, spaced repetition, session analytics, 12-key rotation, a Voice section with pitch-detection feedback; free to try, Pro $4.99/mo; native apps "on the way"; self-described as newer and less tested. — [Fifths comparison](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/) (vendor claims, not independently verified)

#### Functional modules inside broader trainers
- **Toned Ear** (Tone Savvy, iOS, $4.99). Exercises: Intervals, Chords, Scales, Chord Progressions (identify each chord), Perfect Pitch, Scale Degrees (functional: short chord progression then one note), Intervals in Context (two degrees plus the interval), Melodic Dictation (degree of each note of a short melody after a progression). Version 1.0.2, last update 04/26/2018, 2.0/5 from 65 ratings; complaints about no sound/delays (often the silent switch) and sound failing after phone calls; praise: best chord-progression training the reviewer had found. The web version at tonedear.com offers Chord Progressions and Scale Degrees (functional), with teacher assignments and Android/iOS apps; pricing not stated. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/toned-ear/id1353082892), [Tonedear](https://tonedear.com/)
- **EarMaster 7** (Win/Mac/iOS/Android). Chord progression exercise: identify via multiple choice or by naming the step (I, V, bIII) and quality; input interfaces include scale degrees and solfege; Pro 7 claims 2,500+ lessons, voice detection for singing answers, a jazz module; Mac App Store latest 7.8.2 dated 02/10/2025. Prices: Pro 7 $59.95 one-time (family 3-pack $99.95, upgrade $30); resellers differ; Fifths lists $49.95/yr All-Access. Hooktheory's blog says it "centers around classical sheet music notation" and does not mention progressions. — [Search summary of EarMaster shop/listings](https://earmaster.com/shop/product/earmaster-pro-7.html), [Fifths](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/), [Hooktheory blog](https://www.hooktheory.com/blog/ear-training-apps-and-exercises/). Note: the fetched EarMaster software page itself only returned a tagline, so feature claims come from search summaries of the shop page and resellers.
- **Perfect Ear** (iOS/Android). Interval, chord (recognition, inversions, progressions), scale, rhythm (read/write/imitate), melodic dictation, sight reading, absolute pitch and note singing, Circle of Fifths drill, Fretboard Trainer with custom tunings, custom exercise editor; IAP: Premium $14.99, Full Exercise Pack $4.99, editor $1.99 and several $0.99 packs; 4.7/5 from 1.9K; v3.0.9 (Nov 12, year not shown). Reviews: sing-exercise skipping ahead without waiting; wants more difficulty levels; fretboard/custom-exercise range confusion for guitar. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1440768353). No source found showing a key-centred scale-degree method in Perfect Ear.
- **Harmonomics Ear Training** (John Nastos, Apple platforms, $9.99, 4.7/5 from 30). 50+ customizable exercises: intervals, melodies, chords (35+ types, inversions, altered dominants), chord progressions, scales, scale degrees, root movement, "Multiple Notes to Key Center Relationship" with delayed-note playback, Hands-Free mode with spoken answers and auto progression, "palate cleaner" harmonic reset between questions, custom shareable exercises, accuracy/response-time stats, streaks, daily goals, widget. Version 2026.2.1 (Sep 11, year not shown). Complaint: timer pressure/low scores can discourage. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id993814328)
- **Modalissimo Ear Training** (GanSoft, Apple platforms; new, 1 rating). Melody (intervals, scales, modes incl. jazz modes, melodic dictation, sight-singing, microphone singing), Harmony (chords with inversions, progressions, cadences, polyphonic dictation), rhythm, tuner/metronome, Pocket Mode (audio-only with screen locked), adaptive practice, daily goals/streaks/XP, per-interval/chord/degree statistics, on-device mic pitch and rhythm feedback; free is 6 questions/day/exercise, Pro $6.99/mo, $22.99/yr, $34.99 lifetime; v1.2.25 (Oct 3, year not shown). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766067876)
- **OpenEar** (Shachar Har-Shuv, free): Scale Degree Identification, Chord Function Identification (Roman numerals), Common Progressions (with a "Modal Analysis" setting added in 1.12.1, 09/02/2025), Chord Types, Scale Degrees with Chords, Triad Inversions, Intervals. 4.3/5 from 12 ratings. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1616537214)
- **ChordProg Ear Trainer** (DevDa AS): 500+ recorded clips across keys/voicings, toggle chord names vs Roman numerals, replay, levels, results, daily streak; Premium $2.99/mo or $24.99/yr; 4.4/5 from 16 ratings. A reviewer suggests showing all diatonic chords as answer options. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chordprog-ear-trainer-2/id1454739512)
- **Active Ear** (McPherson's Music, free, 4.7/5 from 51): six programs listed by name only: Pitch Recognition, Interval Training, Chord Quality, Bassline Training, Progression Training, Chord Inversions; v1.60.1 (Feb 11, year not shown). Reviews praise a logical order and flexible settings; mechanics of bassline/progression modes are not described on the page. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1541508221)
- **Tenuto / musictheory.net** (Tenuto iOS $4.99; musictheory.net web free): 24 customizable exercises plus calculators; ear-training modes for notes, intervals, scales, chords and keyboard; chord ear training only asks for chord quality per a user review; keyboard/fretboard exercises (note/interval ID, construction). No functional/degree-in-key drill found in the listing. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tenuto/id459313476); vendor-run comparison calls it weak on functional training: [Fifths](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/)
- **Complete Ear Trainer** (Binary Guilt, iOS/Android): game-style progressive drills (counts differ between sources: 150+ drills vs 11 drill types), a beginner mode with 50+ drills, Game Center scores, school leaderboards and teacher custom programs, free with one-time IAP and no subscription. Covers intervals, chords, scales; no functional drill was found in the listing text. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1012455471) (via search summary)
- **Ear Trainer by Songsterr** (free, 4.5/5 from 59, v2.3.5 03/10/2023): hear a melody, match it on a virtual piano; one-two-note phrases growing longer, pentatonic start, backing tracks (blues, swing, bossa nova, funk fusion), live-musician phrases at advanced levels. A reviewer cannot hear the solo lines over blues backing. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1600207375)
- **Hooktheory Chord Crush** (web) - see section 3 for details; Hookpad/TheoryTab are songwriting/database tools, not ear trainers. TheoryTab is a free song database (75,000 songs on one page, "over 50,000" in an older article). — [Chord Crush](https://www.hooktheory.com/chord-crush), [Hooktheory search summaries](https://www.hooktheory.com/blog/producing-resources/)
- **Chordify** (not an ear trainer): automatic chord recognition on songs; loop and slow-down, transposition, capo, MIDI/PDF export are Premium features; live chord detection for guitarists announced; ~$54/yr on one App Store aggregator (pricing sources conflict, not confirmed on an official page); users report wrong chords and timing issues. — [GuitarChalk review](https://www.guitarchalk.com/chordify-review/), [MusicRadar](https://musicradar.com/news/chordify-lyrics-live-chord-detection)

No degree/functional drill was found for either. — [Producerhive comparison](https://producerhive.com/ask-the-hive/rocksmith-vs-yousician/), [Ubisoft dev diary](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rocksmith/plus/news-updates/1Zcwvdcw0IwhBEYoCGCQtt/rocksmith-dev-diary-february-2022-audio-updates)

- **Musical U**: its scales module includes "Scale Degree Recognition" on the major pentatonic (do, re, mi, so, la). — [Musical U](https://www.musical-u.com/modules/melodies/ear-expansion-scales)

### Inferences
- Within this sample, only Sonofield and Fifths (the latter vendor-claimed) make the drone plus scale-degree plus sing-back loop their core; most others bolt a "scale degrees" module onto a broad curriculum.
- Pricing norms: lifetime unlock $14.99-$34.99 (Sonofield, FET Plus, Perfect Ear, Modalissimo), subscriptions $22.99-$79/yr (Modalissimo, ChordProg, Chord Crush, Fifths), desktop one-time $59.95 (EarMaster).

### Gaps
- Not found/verified: Earbeater, Fretello, Sonid, Interval Mentor, Relative Pitch Master, Perfect Pitch Pro, Chord Tracker, Solfege Trainer, "Ear Trainer by Mobile Software", Auralia (only a vendor mention as "expensive conservatory tool"), "Functional Ear Training" by Ear Training Co. (the App Store id6766067876 returned is actually Modalissimo, so no Ear Training Co. app was confirmed). Searches for Earbeater and Fretello returned nothing about these names.
- No data on Google Play ratings, install counts or review text.
- Tonedear pricing and Complete Ear Trainer pricing figures are from summaries, not fetched pages.

## 2. How is feedback and correction done (replay, sing back, show resolution)? Which approaches are common versus rare?

### Takeaway
Replay of the question and reveal of the correct answer are universal; "resolution" of the degree to the tonic as a correction is not described in any listing found. Sing-back with pitch detection is present in only a few (Sonofield, StringKick, EarMaster Pro, Modalissimo, Perfect Ear note-singing, Fifths), and instrument play-along grading is essentially absent from ear trainers (users ask for it).

### Cited Findings
- Table stakes (appear in nearly every listing): cadence/drone to set the key, multiple-choice answer buttons, replay, per-item statistics, daily streak/goal, custom exercise settings. — e.g. [FET](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926), [Harmonomics](https://apps.apple.com/app/id993814328), [Modalissimo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766067876), [ChordProg](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chordprog-ear-trainer-2/id1454739512)
- Drone instead of a cadence: Sonofield (sustained drone, volume can be zero, optional melodic cadence on key change) and StringKick. — [Sonofield](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sonofield-ear-trainer/id6740409139), [StringKick](https://www.stringkick.com/scale-degree-ear-trainer/)
- Sing-back graded by microphone pitch detection: Sonofield Voice Mode, StringKick singing mode, EarMaster Pro voice detection, Modalissimo microphone feedback, Perfect Ear note singing (with complaints that it skips ahead without waiting), Fifths Voice section (vendor claim). — same sources as section 1; Perfect Ear complaint in [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1440768353)
- Hands-free/audio-only modes: Harmonomics Hands-Free (spoken answers), Sonofield Pocket Mode, Modalissimo Pocket Mode. — as above
- Chord Crush feedback: rating falls on misses and the puzzle type adjusts after a few misses; at lower levels you can click chords to hear them and this help is removed as rating improves; the page does not describe other correction on wrong answers. Modes: Train (adaptive), Level Up, Rush (timed), Daily Challenge, Customize and Missed Chords (the latter two paid). — [Chord Crush page](https://www.hooktheory.com/chord-crush), [Hooktheory blog (promotional, ~2024)](https://www.hooktheory.com/blog/ear-training-apps-and-exercises/)
- Play-along on instrument graded by pitch detection exists in Yousician-type products (microphone-based note detection) but not as a degree/functional drill in the listings found. A bassist FET user requested exactly this (detect notes played on the instrument). — [Producerhive](https://producerhive.com/ask-the-hive/rocksmith-vs-yousician/), [FET App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926)
- Real-song material: Chord Crush puzzles come from real songs in TheoryTab with chords missing, identified by scale degree. ChordProg uses 500+ recorded clips (not stated as real songs). Chordify gives chords for arbitrary songs with loop/slow-down but is not a trainer. — [Chord Crush](https://www.hooktheory.com/chord-crush), [ChordProg](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chordprog-ear-trainer-2/id1454739512), [GuitarChalk](https://www.guitarchalk.com/chordify-review/)
- Adaptivity: Chord Crush Elo-like rating and Premium "custom sets that target blind spots" ($79/yr); Modalissimo "adaptive practice" and per-degree stats; Fifths spaced repetition (vendor claim); FET custom levels and per-note accuracy; Harmonomics stats but no adaptivity described. — [Chord Crush pricing](https://www.hooktheory.com/chord-crush/pricing), [Modalissimo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766067876), [Fifths](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/)
- Notation/naming options: FET letters/numbers/solfege; EarMaster scale degrees or solfege; ChordProg toggles chord names vs Roman numerals; Chord Crush shows degrees (Roman numerals optional); OpenEar uses Roman numerals. — sources above
- Key-change handling: Sonofield melodic cadence on key change; FET custom cadence per level and a random-major mode (anecdotal); Fifths 12-key rotation (vendor). — [Sonofield](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sonofield-ear-trainer/id6740409139), [Substack comment](https://jerkmakesmusic.substack.com/p/how-to-develop-your-sense-of-pitch/comments)
- Gamification: Complete Ear Trainer (Game Center, leaderboards), Modalissimo XP/streaks, Tonegym streaks and leaderboards (vendor-run source), Chord Crush Rush timed mode, Harmonomics 3D achievements. The timer in Harmonomics drew a stress complaint. — [Harmonomics](https://apps.apple.com/app/id993814328), [Fifths](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/)

### Inferences
- Common (table stakes): cadence/drone-plus-note, multiple choice, stats and streaks, notation toggles.
- Rarer: singing graded by mic, hands-free spoken-answer sessions, key-change cadence option, adaptive weak-item sets, real-song progression puzzles with degree answers (Chord Crush is the one clear example found).
- Not found in any listing: explicit "resolve the note to the tonic" as post-answer correction, bass-line-by-degree drills with real voicings (Active Ear lists "Bassline Training" but without detail), melody dictation in degrees combined with guitar fretboard grading, and "slow down real song and transcribe by ear" inside an ear trainer. Absence from listings is not proof of absence from the apps.

### Gaps
- Correction behavior (does the app replay the right answer in context? sing it? show resolution) is mostly not stated on listings; would need hands-on testing.
- Pitch-shift/microtonal key changes: nothing found.
- Whether Chord Crush's "YouTube" integration exists: a testimonial mentions it, the product page does not describe it; unconfirmed.

## 3. Common user complaints about functional ear trainers (plateau, losing the key, boring, no transfer to songs) and how apps tried to address them

### Takeaway
Direct, citable complaint evidence is thin (no Reddit access). What is visible: reliability/sound bugs, shallow scope and lack of depth, pressure from timers, need for real-instrument input, and a theoretical/vendor argument that interval drills don't transfer to music. Remedies seen in products: drone, cadence on key change, real-song puzzles, hands-free listening, singing, custom weak-spot sets.

### Cited Findings
- Sound failures/silent-switch problems and audio after phone calls (Toned Ear); NULL errors (ChordProg); sung-note exercise skipping ahead (Perfect Ear). — [Toned Ear](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/toned-ear/id1353082892), [ChordProg](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chordprog-ear-trainer-2/id1454739512), [Perfect Ear](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1440768353)
- Narrow scope: FET reviewer "limits its scope but delivers results"; vendor-run article calls it a single-task app. A Perfect Ear reviewer wants more difficulty levels and depth. — [FET](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926), [Fifths](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/best-ear-training-apps/), [Perfect Ear](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1440768353)
- Timed pressure and discouraging scores (Harmonomics reviewer, 2020), who nevertheless calls the scores an honest measure. — [Harmonomics](https://apps.apple.com/app/id993814328)
- Wanting harder answer sets: ChordProg reviewer suggests showing all diatonic chords as options. — [ChordProg](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chordprog-ear-trainer-2/id1454739512)
- Instrument link missing: bassist wants note detection on the real instrument. — [FET](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1088761926)
- Theory of transfer: functional recognition "fails without a clear tonic" and "key changes require constant re-analysis" (Wikipedia); familiar-melody associations are "quite limited in scope" (Rogers 1983 cited there). — [Wikipedia: Ear training](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ear_training)
- Vendor argument (Fifths, May 2026, promotional): song mnemonics for intervals stop helping in real music; accurate interval drilling can still fail to identify a vi chord; recommends each question be placed in an established key and a sequence from tonic-finding to degrees to intervals to chords to dictation; admits its own evidence is practitioner consensus, not controlled comparison. — [Fifths blog](https://fifths-marketing.pages.dev/blog/functional-vs-interval-ear-training/)
- Remedies observed: drone to keep tonic present (Sonofield, StringKick); cadence on each key change (Sonofield); random key with cadence (FET, anecdotal); real-song chord puzzles with fading hints (Chord Crush); hands-free passive listening (Harmonomics, Sonofield, Modalissimo); mic singing to engage production (Sonofield, StringKick, EarMaster); adaptive/missed-item sets (Chord Crush Premium, Modalissimo, Fifths). — sources in sections 1-2

### Inferences
- "Losing the key" is addressed mainly by drones/cadences and key-change cadences; none of the listings describes detecting that the learner has drifted.
- "Plateau/boredom" is addressed by gamification (timers, XP) which has its own complaint (pressure), and by real-song content which only Chord Crush clearly offers; passive/hands-free modes target low-effort repetition.
- Transfer to real songs remains an argued claim: no controlled evidence was found in these sources.

### Gaps
- No r/eartraining, r/musictheory, r/guitarlessons, Hacker News or YouTube evidence was retrieved (Reddit blocked for fetch; searches returned none). Complaints about plateau/boredom/transfer in the user's own words are therefore unverified here.
- No Google Play review content was retrieved.
- No LLM/AI-based music ear trainer was found: a search returned only hearing-loss auditory training products (Lace AI Pro, Tinnitus Pro) and nothing for music pitch/functional training.
