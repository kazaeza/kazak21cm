Chordonomicon — chord progressions (CC BY-NC 4.0)

The harmony of the extra pieces on the «мои лайки» shelf of the built-in songbook (O-11, ADR-033; LikesShelf in
SongBookLikes.kt) comes from the open Chordonomicon dataset: community chord charts of 666,000 songs, each with the
Spotify track id, the genre and the section names of the chart. Only the chord symbols are used (no lyrics, no melody),
and only as degrees of a tonic that was worked out separately, simplified. The comment above each piece is the row id of
the chart in the dataset file (chordonomicon_v2.csv).

The dataset is released under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/): attribution is required, commercial use is not allowed. The app is a
personal sideloaded build that is not sold. If it is ever distributed commercially, this shelf has to be removed or
re-sourced first. The authors ask users to cite:

  Spyridon Kantarelis, Konstantinos Thomas, Vassilis Lyberatos, Edmund Dervakos and Giorgos Stamou. "CHORDONOMICON: A
  Dataset of 666,000 Songs and their Chord Progressions." arXiv preprint arXiv:2410.22046, 2024.
  https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.22046

Dataset page: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ailsntua/Chordonomicon

The tonic of a piece is not part of the dataset. It was chosen from the chart itself and the key that Spotify reports for
the track. The keys were looked up by track id in two public tables of audio features on Hugging Face
(GildasLeDrogoff/spotify-huge-track-analysis-dataset, CC BY-NC 4.0; maharshipandya/spotify-tracks-dataset, BSD),
on the build machine only. Nothing from those tables, and no Spotify track id, is stored in this repository.
