McGill Billboard Project — chord annotations (CC0 1.0)

The harmony on the «хиты 60–80-х» shelf of the built-in songbook (ADR-032) comes from the expert annotations of the
McGill Billboard Project: bar-by-bar chord labels, the tonic and the section names of 890 recordings that charted in the
Billboard Hot 100 between 1958 and 1991. Only the annotations are used (no audio, no lyrics, no melody), and only as
degrees of the annotated tonic, simplified.

The annotations are released under the Creative Commons Zero 1.0 licence (public domain dedication). The authors ask
users to follow scholarly norms and cite:

  John Ashley Burgoyne, Jonathan Wild and Ichiro Fujinaga. "An Expert Ground-Truth Set for Audio Chord Recognition and
  Music Analysis." Proceedings of the 12th International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference (ISMIR),
  Miami, 2011.

  John Ashley Burgoyne. "Stochastic Processes and Database-Driven Musicology." PhD dissertation, McGill University, 2012.

Project page: https://ddmal.music.mcgill.ca/research/The_McGill_Billboard_Project_(Chord_Analysis_Dataset)
